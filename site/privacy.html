Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Lurk collects no personal data. It never reads, stores or sends your messages. Its settings and counts stay in your browser, and there is no account.

This policy covers the Lurk browser extension for Chrome, Firefox and Safari ("the extension") and this website ("the website").

What the extension collects

Nothing. The extension has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. It never sends any data off your device.

What the extension looks at

On the supported chat apps only, the extension looks at the requests the app is about to send, to recognise a read receipt, a story view or a typing signal. When it finds one, it changes or stops that request in your browser. It does not read, keep or send the content of your messages, stories or contacts.

What the extension stores on your device

The extension keeps a few values in your browser's own extension storage. They never leave your device:

  • whether Lurk is switched on, and which signals to hide (Seen, story views, typing);
  • your light or dark theme for the popup;
  • how many Seen receipts, story views and typing signals it hid on each app, and the date counting started;
  • when a 10-second "let receipts through" pass ends, while one is running.

"Reset stats" in the popup clears the counts. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Why the extension needs its permissions

  • Access to the supported apps (chat.google.com, facebook.com, messenger.com, instagram.com, web.whatsapp.com, teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, teams.cloud.microsoft): to run there and hide the signals described above. It does nothing on any other site.
  • declarativeNetRequest: to block the Google Chat and Teams read-receipt requests and the Google Chat typing request with the browser's built-in rules.
  • storage: to keep the settings and counts listed above.
  • alarms: to turn hiding back on when a 10-second pass ends.
  • activeTab: to show in the popup which app the open tab is on. The address is read on your device when you open the popup, and never stored or sent.

The website

The website has no cookies, no sign-up and no analytics, and its fonts are served from the website itself. The company hosting it may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. The store links lead to the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons or the Mac App Store, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The extension and the website collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
