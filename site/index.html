Questions

Can the other person tell I read it?

Not from a receipt: they see the message as delivered. Replying does count as reading, though. Sending a message marks the chat read, and no extension can stop that.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Lurk covers the web versions of these apps in your desktop browser. The phone and desktop apps, and replying from a notification, still send receipts.

Will chats still work normally?

Yes. Lurk answers the app as if the receipt went out, so nothing waits or retries. One side effect: chats you open stay unread for you, on every device, until you reply or let a receipt through.

Is it free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT licence. No account, no upsell.

What if an app changes?

Lurk works with how each app sends receipts today. If an app changes that, hiding can stop on that app until Lurk is updated.

Can I see the code?

Yes. All of it is on GitHub at botjaeger/lurk, under the MIT licence.

Privacy

Lurk collects nothing. It never reads or stores your messages, and nothing leaves your browser. Its settings and counts stay on your device.
